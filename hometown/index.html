My Hometown

Jakarta, officially known as the Special Capital Region of Jakarta (DKI Jakarta) and formerly known as Batavia, is the capital of Indonesia and an autonomous region at the provincial level. Jakarta comprises five administrative cities and one administrative regency. DKI Jakarta is classified as a metropolitan city and is nicknamed “The Big Durian” because it is considered comparable to New York City (the “Big Apple”) in the United States.

Jakarta covers an area of approximately 664.01 km² (including 6,977.5 km² of ocean), with a population of 11,038,216 as of the end of 2024. As a center for business, politics, and culture, Jakarta is home to the headquarters of state-owned enterprises, private companies, and foreign firms. The city is also home to government institutions and the ASEAN Secretariat. Jakarta is served by two airports Soekarno-Hatta International Airport in Tangerang, Banten, and Halim Perdanakusuma Airport as well as two seaports: Tanjung Priok and Sunda Kelapa.
